<p class="lead">An online paper series where IS scholars discuss papers, ideally with their authors, with 40+ sessions in 2024&ndash;2025. Faculty and practitioners are welcome. Our motto: one more critique makes the paper better.</p>
<p class="lead">Weibo Li (ASU) now leads the group. To join or present, email <a href="mailto:weiboli@asu.edu">weiboli@asu.edu</a>.</p>
